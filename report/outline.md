# Report outline

A working outline to fill in as experiments finish:

1. **Introduction** — Why early learning support matters and what we want to predict.
2. **Data** — EdNet, solving rounds, cleaning, and which students have enough records.
3. **Exploration** — History lengths, frequent questions and tags, response times, and risk-label counts.
4. **Models** — Baselines, DKT, SAKT, and the shared training setup.
5. **Results** — Next-answer and risk prediction, with variation across seeds and confidence intervals.
6. **Generalization** — Unseen bundles, short histories, and probability calibration.
7. **Discussion** — Who gets flagged, who gets missed, and what a limited tutoring budget would mean.
8. **Limitations** — Question difficulty, missing outcomes, missing demographics, and what the logs cannot establish about learning or interventions.
9. **Reproduction** — Data version, commands, environment, and saved run settings.
